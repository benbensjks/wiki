# HZH混合三级模型：核心方程与符号
# Core Equations and Notation for the HZH Hybrid Three-Bit Model

本附录对应HBY bit0 → ZMH现行A0/F0与bit1 → HBY A1/F1与bit2的34态接收模型。所有导数以小时为时间单位；a.u.保留各模块的原模型标尺。本附录不适用于HZZ或独立51态模型的完整系统。

This appendix specifies the 34-state receiving system comprising HBY bit0, the current ZMH A0/F0 and bit1 module, and HBY A1/F1 and bit2. All derivatives use hours as the time unit. Arbitrary units (a.u.) retain the original model scales of the respective modules. These equations do not specify the complete HZZ or independent 51-state system.

## 1. 状态、位序与Hill函数 / States, Bit Order, and Hill Functions

对HBY两端位$i\in\{0,2\}$，$I_i$、$T_i$、$R_i$分别表示成熟游离整合酶、位内阻遏蛋白和RDF；$C_i$为显式Int–RDF复合物，$S_i$为LR比例。$M$表示mRNA，上标$u$表示未成熟蛋白。ZMH中位直接积分$PB_1$及成熟蛋白$I_1,T_1,R_1$。$F_0,F_1$是进位抑制因子，不是$R_i$。

For the HBY bits $i\in\{0,2\}$, $I_i$, $T_i$, and $R_i$ denote mature free integrase, the within-bit repressor, and RDF, respectively. $C_i$ is an explicit Int–RDF complex and $S_i$ is the LR fraction. $M$ denotes mRNA and superscript $u$ denotes immature protein. The ZMH middle bit directly integrates $PB_1$ and the mature proteins $I_1,T_1,R_1$. Carry inhibitors $F_0,F_1$ are distinct from the RDF pools $R_i$.

$$
PB_i=1-S_i\quad(i=0,2),\qquad S_1=1-PB_1,
\qquad
H(x;K,n)=\frac{x^n}{K^n+x^n},\quad G=1-H.
$$

三个HBY/ZMH位与两级carry的状态数分别为$11+(2+4)+(6+11)=34$。数学变量集不用于推断实现索引；代码顺序以`model_hzh.NAMES`为准。整数读数使用窗内数字标签$b_i\in\{0,1\}$，$N=b_0+2b_1+4b_2$，不直接将连续$S_i$相加当作整数。

The state count is $11+(2+4)+(6+11)=34$. The mathematical variable sets should not be used to infer implementation indices; the code order is defined by `model_hzh.NAMES`. The integer count is decoded from the within-window labels $b_i\in\{0,1\}$ as $N=b_0+2b_1+4b_2$, rather than by directly summing continuous DNA fractions.

## 2. HBY表达链与产生源换算 / HBY Expression Chains and Source Mapping

除bit0整合酶的上游同源输入外，HBY表达链使用：

Except for the upstream-derived integrase input to bit0, HBY expression chains use:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dot M_X&=b_X-\lambda_m M_X,\\
\dot X^u&=k_{\rm tl}M_X-k_{{\rm mat},X}X^u,\\
\dot X&=k_{{\rm mat},X}X^u-\gamma_X X,\\
b_X&=u_X\frac{\lambda_m}{k_{\rm tl}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

这里$u_X$是目标成熟产生速率。选定配置的未成熟态额外损失为0，故未成熟总损失等于成熟速率；一般映射$b_X=u_X\lambda_m\lambda_u/(k_{\rm tl}k_{\rm mat})$因而简化为上式。前体达到恒定输入稳态时，$k_{\rm mat}X^u=u_X$。Int和RDF的成熟态还要加入第5节的结合项。

Here, $u_X$ is a target mature-protein production rate. Additional immature-protein loss is zero in the selected configuration, so the total immature-state loss equals the maturation rate. The general mapping $b_X=u_X\lambda_m\lambda_u/(k_{\rm tl}k_{\rm mat})$ therefore reduces to the expression above. At precursor steady state under constant input, $k_{\rm mat}X^u=u_X$. The mature integrase and RDF equations additionally include the binding terms in Section 5.

取$\lambda_m=\ln2\times60/2$ h$^{-1}$，$k_{\rm tl}=30$ h$^{-1}$。位内蛋白的$k_{\rm mat}=\ln2\times60/20$ h$^{-1}$，carry1中A1/F1的$k_{\rm mat}=\ln2\times60/32.5$ h$^{-1}$。成熟态总清除率按原表使用，下游不再额外加入稀释。

The selected values are $\lambda_m=\ln2\times60/2$ h$^{-1}$ and $k_{\rm tl}=30$ h$^{-1}$. The maturation rate is $\ln2\times60/20$ h$^{-1}$ for within-bit proteins and $\ln2\times60/32.5$ h$^{-1}$ for A1/F1 in carry1. Mature-protein clearance rates retain their tabulated total-loss interpretation, without an additional downstream dilution term.

## 3. HBY位内产生源与第二级carry / HBY Within-Bit Sources and the Second Carry Stage

以下源项进入第2节的相应表达链。$PB_1$来自ZMH中位，$I_0$来自同一混合电路的bit0：

The following sources enter their respective expression chains in Section 2. $PB_1$ is supplied by the ZMH middle bit and $I_0$ by bit0 of the same hybrid circuit:

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_i^T&=3.5(1-S_i),\quad
u_i^R=6S_iG(T_i;0.85,3.9),\quad i\in\{0,2\},\\
u^A_1&=16PB_1G(A_1;0.6,2),\\
u^F_1&=5H(A_1;1.2,4),\\
g_1&=H(A_1;1.2,6)G(F_1;0.4,4)H(I_0;0.3,2),\\
u_2^I&=38g_1.
\end{aligned}
$$

源项单位为a.u./h。$T_i$与上游振荡器TetR不同。A1的负自馈在本HZH配置中开启；F1产生指数4与Int2门激活指数6分开使用。A1/F1的清除率分别为2.5、1.1 h$^{-1}$，HBY位内$T_i$与$R_i$分别为0.6、0.8 h$^{-1}$。

Sources are expressed in a.u./h. $T_i$ is distinct from upstream oscillator TetR. A1 negative autoregulation is enabled in this HZH configuration. The F1 production exponent of 4 and Int2 activation-gate exponent of 6 are applied separately. Clearance rates are 2.5 and 1.1 h$^{-1}$ for A1 and F1, and 0.6 and 0.8 h$^{-1}$ for within-bit HBY repressors and RDFs.

## 4. bit0的C31同源输入 / The Upstream-Derived C31 Input to Bit0

设$h_{31}(60t)$为按分钟求解的上游C31启动子活性，$N_o$为振荡器质粒拷贝数。$\kappa=602.214076\times5.75$ molecules/a.u.按1 fL参考体积定义。bit0的转录本与上游C31 mRNA同源，使用下式而非再次施加目标产生速率换算：

Let $h_{31}(60t)$ denote the upstream C31 promoter activity computed on a minute-based time axis, and $N_o$ the oscillator plasmid copy number. The conversion factor $\kappa=602.214076\times5.75$ molecules/a.u. is defined using a 1 fL reference volume. The bit0 transcript represents the upstream C31 mRNA and follows the equations below, rather than an additional target-production-rate mapping:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dot M_0^I&=\frac{60N_ok_{\rm tx,31}h_{31}(60t)}{\kappa}
-60(\delta_{m,31}+\mu_{\rm up})M_0^I,\\
\dot I_0^u&=60\beta_{31}M_0^I-k_{{\rm mat},I}I_0^u.
\end{aligned}
$$

其中$k_{\rm tx,31}=5$ transcripts·plasmid$^{-1}$·min$^{-1}$、$\beta_{31}=0.5$ min$^{-1}$；原上游mRNA总损失满足$\delta_{m,31}+\mu_{\rm up}=\ln2/2$ min$^{-1}$。该路径没有额外乘方波系数$k_{\rm int}=6$。上游与接收端采用单向连接。

Here, $k_{\rm tx,31}=5$ transcripts·plasmid$^{-1}$·min$^{-1}$, $\beta_{31}=0.5$ min$^{-1}$, and the upstream total mRNA loss satisfies $\delta_{m,31}+\mu_{\rm up}=\ln2/2$ min$^{-1}$. No additional square-wave coefficient $k_{\rm int}=6$ is applied. Coupling from the upstream model to the receiving system is unidirectional.

## 5. HBY游离池与复合物 / HBY Free Pools and Explicit Complexes

对$i\in\{0,2\}$，成熟Int/RDF与复合物满足：

For $i\in\{0,2\}$, mature integrase, RDF, and complex dynamics are:

$$
\begin{aligned}
B_i&=k_{\rm on}I_iR_i,\quad D_i=k_{\rm off}C_i,\\
\dot I_i&=k_{{\rm mat},I}I_i^u-\gamma_{{\rm int},i}I_i-B_i+D_i,\\
\dot R_i&=k_{{\rm mat},R}R_i^u-0.8R_i-B_i+D_i,\\
\dot C_i&=B_i-D_i-\delta_CC_i,\\
q&=\frac{k_{\rm on}}{k_{\rm off}+\delta_C},\quad
K_C=qK_{D,\rm comp},\\
C_i\approx qI_iR_i&\ \Longrightarrow\ H(C_i;qK_{D,\rm comp},2)
\approx H(I_iR_i;K_{D,\rm comp},2).
\end{aligned}
$$

取$k_{\rm on}=0.1$ a.u.$^{-1}$h$^{-1}$、$k_{\rm off}=\delta_C=1$ h$^{-1}$；$q=0.05$ a.u.$^{-1}$、$K_{D,\rm comp}=1.2$ a.u.$^2$，得到$K_C=0.06$ a.u.。$\gamma_{{\rm int},0}=2$、$\gamma_{{\rm int},2}=2.2$ h$^{-1}$。准稳态近似只匹配反向Hill响应，不能使两个完整模型动态等价。DNA重组本身不另设RDF消耗项。

The selected values are $k_{\rm on}=0.1$ a.u.$^{-1}$h$^{-1}$ and $k_{\rm off}=\delta_C=1$ h$^{-1}$, yielding $q=0.05$ a.u.$^{-1}$. With $K_{D,\rm comp}=1.2$ a.u.$^2$, $K_C=0.06$ a.u. Integrase clearance rates are $\gamma_{{\rm int},0}=2$ and $\gamma_{{\rm int},2}=2.2$ h$^{-1}$. The quasi-steady-state approximation matches the reverse Hill response only; it does not establish dynamic equivalence of the complete models. DNA recombination has no additional stoichiometric RDF-consumption term.

## 6. HBY bit0与bit2的DNA重组 / DNA Recombination in HBY Bit0 and Bit2

两端的正向指数均为2，正向半饱和参数分别为$K_{I,0}=1.0$、$K_{I,2}=0.6$ a.u.：

Both HBY bits use a forward Hill exponent of 2, with half-activation parameters $K_{I,0}=1.0$ and $K_{I,2}=0.6$ a.u.:

$$
\begin{aligned}
J_{{\rm fwd},i}&=7(1-S_i)H(I_i;K_{I,i},2)\frac{0.1}{0.1+R_i},\\
J_{{\rm rev},i}&=5S_iH(C_i;0.06,2),\\
\dot S_i&=J_{{\rm fwd},i}-J_{{\rm rev},i},\quad i\in\{0,2\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

完整DNA分数通量$J$的单位为h$^{-1}$。正向反应受到游离RDF抑制；反向反应由显式复合物驱动。

The complete DNA-fraction fluxes $J$ have units of h$^{-1}$. Free RDF inhibits the forward reaction, while the explicit complex drives the reverse reaction.

## 7. ZMH第一级carry与中位蛋白 / ZMH First-Stage Carry and Middle-Bit Proteins

以下系数由《完整二级级联.py》的分钟速率乘60得到，阈值和指数不随时间单位改变：

The rates below are obtained by multiplying the minute-based rates in `完整二级级联.py` by 60. Thresholds and exponents are unaffected by the time-unit conversion:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dot A_0&=8.478PB_0-4.386A_0,\\
\dot F_0&=5.874H(A_0;1.0,2)-0.696F_0,\\
g_0&=H(A_0;1.0,2)G(F_0;2.6454,5.4332),\\
\dot I_1&=22.728g_0-4.8I_1,\\
\dot T_1&=12PB_1-0.3T_1,\\
\dot R_1&=1.59S_1G(T_1;1.8,3)-0.3R_1.
\end{aligned}
$$

$F_0$在原二级代码中记为r0／R0、在混合模型中记为F0_zmh。$T_1$和$R_1$分别是Rep1和RDF1。以上均为直接蛋白方程，没有显式mRNA、未成熟态或额外成熟延迟。

$F_0$ is denoted r0/R0 in the original two-bit code and F0_zmh in the hybrid model. $T_1$ and $R_1$ denote Rep1 and RDF1, respectively. These are direct protein-level equations, without explicit mRNA, immature states, or an additional maturation delay.

## 8. ZMH bit1的DNA重组 / DNA Recombination in ZMH Bit1

中位正向指数为4，反向驱动使用同一时刻的$I_1R_1$，不是独立峰值相乘：

The middle bit uses a forward exponent of 4. Reverse recombination is driven by the simultaneous product $I_1R_1$, rather than the product of independently determined peaks:

$$
\begin{aligned}
J_{{\rm fwd},1}&=7.08PB_1H(I_1;1.8,4)\frac{0.1026}{0.1026+R_1},\\
J_{{\rm rev},1}&=4.8S_1H(I_1R_1;3.2,2),\\
\dot{PB}_1&=-J_{{\rm fwd},1}+J_{{\rm rev},1},\qquad S_1=1-PB_1.
\end{aligned}
$$

此处3.2的量纲为a.u.$^2$，作用于Int与RDF的乘积；HBY反向参数0.06 a.u.作用于复合物浓度。两者不是可直接比较的相同物理量。

The threshold 3.2 has units of a.u.$^2$ and acts on the integrase–RDF product, whereas the HBY reverse threshold 0.06 a.u. acts on complex concentration. These are different quantities and cannot be directly compared as a common physical threshold.

## 来源与使用范围 / Sources and Scope

方程对应[model_hzh.py](hby_zmh_hby/model_hzh.py)与[hybrid_model.py](hybrid_model.py)的HZH实现。基础数值、源版本与初始化记录见[主结果完整参数与来源](hby_zmh_hby/主结果_完整参数与来源.md)。韩上游输入按分钟计算，再按上述方式接入小时制ODE；C31蛋白浓度不作为本HZH bit0的外加强迫状态。

The equations correspond to the HZH implementation in [model_hzh.py](hby_zmh_hby/model_hzh.py) and [hybrid_model.py](hybrid_model.py). Baseline values, source versions, and initialization are documented in the [complete parameter and provenance record](hby_zmh_hby/主结果_完整参数与来源.md). The minute-based upstream input is coupled to the hour-based receiving ODEs as specified above. C31 protein concentration is not imposed as an external forcing state on HZH bit0.

这些公式是有效动力学模型，未展开全部DNA结合、联会和催化中间态；浓度换算系数及新增表达／复合物参数仍需实验标定。上游与下游生长条件的一致性问题仍保留。

These are effective kinetic equations and do not resolve every DNA-binding, synapsis, or catalytic intermediate. The concentration-conversion factor and added expression/complex parameters require experimental calibration. Consistency between the upstream and downstream growth conditions remains unresolved.
